SAFETY & CAUTIONS

  • Do not look directly into the red light LEDs while the device is operating.
  • Do not use on broken, irritated, or sensitive skin.
  • Keep away from water and excessive moisture.
  • Discontinue use if discomfort, irritation, or overheating occurs.
  • Consult a healthcare professional before use if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or injury in the treatment area, have an implanted medical device such as a pacemaker, or have light sensitivity.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
